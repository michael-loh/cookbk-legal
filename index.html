Information we handle

Account details. If you sign up or sign in, we use your email address, password, account identifier and session information to authenticate you and connect your saved content to your account. Supabase provides account authentication. Session information is also stored on your device or browser to keep you signed in.

Recipes and photos. We handle recipes you enter or save, including names, descriptions, ingredients, instructions, notes, tags, cooking times, servings, nutrition estimates, source links and photos. When cloud services are configured, recipes and retained photos are stored with Supabase so they can be accessed across devices. Some content is also kept locally on your device or browser.

AI requests. When you ask CookBk to analyze a photo, import a recipe or answer a cooking question, the request may include a selected photo, written hints, messages, a previous recipe proposal, an Instagram link or post text. Cooking chat also sends summaries of your saved recipes to help the assistant answer in the context of your recipe box. Those summaries may include recipe names, ingredients, descriptions, tags, cooking times, servings and nutrition estimates.

Imported posts. If you submit an Instagram reel link, we may retrieve publicly available caption, creator attribution and image information to make a recipe draft. CookBk does not ask for your Instagram password.

Subscriptions and usage. We handle account identifiers, subscription status, purchase or transaction identifiers and renewal dates to provide Pro access and restore purchases. We record daily AI usage counts to apply free usage limits. Apple or Google processes payment through its app store; RevenueCat manages subscription information for CookBk.

Operational records. Our services create request identifiers, timestamps, account email addresses, durations and error logs for troubleshooting and operating CookBk. In some error cases, logs may include recipe details or other content associated with the failed request.

How we use information

  • To create accounts and keep you signed in.
  • To save, display and synchronize recipes and photos.
  • To produce recipe drafts and respond to cooking questions you submit.
  • To import content from links you choose to provide.
  • To provide Pro features, restore purchases and apply usage limits.
  • To remember local preferences and conversation history.
  • To troubleshoot errors, protect and maintain the service.

Services that process information

  • Supabase processes account authentication, cloud recipes and photos, synchronization and backend requests.
  • Google Gemini processes AI requests. Depending on the feature, this can include photos, messages, imported text and summaries of saved recipes.
  • Apify may retrieve post information from Instagram links you submit when automatic importing is available.
  • RevenueCat manages subscription entitlements and purchase reporting. Apple or Google processes the store transaction.
