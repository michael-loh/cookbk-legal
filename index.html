CookBk Privacy Policy

Prepared October 22, 2026

CookBk is operated by Michael Loh, an individual based in New York, United States ("CookBk," "we," "us"). This policy explains how we handle information when you use our recipe app, website, AI features, and support services. CookBk's initial release is intended for adults in the United States. Contact us about support or privacy at support@cookbk.app.

Information we handle

Account information. We use your email address, password, account identifier, and session information to create and authenticate your account and connect it to your saved content. Supabase provides authentication. Session information is also stored on your device or browser to keep you signed in.

Recipes and photos. We handle recipes you enter or save, including titles, descriptions, ingredients, instructions, notes, tags, cooking times, servings, nutrition estimates, source links, and photos. Saved recipes and uploaded photos are stored with Supabase for access across devices. Some content, preferences, and pending shared links are also stored on your device or browser.

AI requests. When you ask CookBk to analyze a photo, import a recipe, or answer a cooking question, we send the information needed for that feature to Google Gemini through our backend. This can include your selected photo, written hints, chat messages and conversation context, a previous recipe proposal, an Instagram link, or imported post text. Cooking chat also sends summaries of saved recipes to help answer in the context of your recipe box. Summaries can include recipe titles, ingredients, descriptions, tags, cooking times, servings, and nutrition estimates.

Imported posts. When you submit an Instagram link, we may use Apify to retrieve publicly available captions, creator attribution, and image information to prepare a recipe draft. We do not ask for your Instagram password.

Subscriptions and usage. We and RevenueCat process account identifiers, subscription status, purchase or transaction identifiers, and renewal information to provide Pro access and restore purchases. We record daily AI usage counts to apply usage limits. Apple or Google processes purchases made through its app store; CookBk does not receive your full payment-card details from those purchases.

Technical and operational information. Hosting, backend, and subscription services may process IP addresses, device and browser information, request identifiers, timestamps, connection metadata, and error information. Our backend logs can include account email addresses, request durations, recipe titles, and, in some error cases, recipe content or other details associated with the failed request.

Support messages. If you contact us, we receive your email address, message, attachments, and information needed to investigate and respond. Please avoid including passwords, payment-card details, or unrelated sensitive information.

How we use information

  • Create accounts, authenticate users, and maintain sessions.
  • Save, display, organize, and synchronize recipes and photos.
  • Generate recipe drafts and answer cooking questions you submit.
  • Retrieve post information from links you choose to import.
  • Provide subscription features, restore purchases, and enforce usage limits.
  • Remember preferences and local conversation history.
  • Respond to support and privacy requests, troubleshoot problems, protect the service, and meet legal obligations.

Services that process information

  • Supabase: account authentication, database records, uploaded photos, synchronization, backend functions, backups, and service logs.
  • Google Gemini: AI processing of the inputs and context described above. CookBk uses a billing-enabled paid API project, with optional request logging and Contributor Tier disabled, and does not share API datasets with Google. Under Google's paid-service terms, prompts and responses are not used to improve Google's products. Separate abuse-monitoring processing still applies, including possible review of flagged content. See Google's API terms and abuse-monitoring policy.
  • Apify: processing of submitted Instagram URLs and retrieved post information for recipe importing.
  • RevenueCat, Apple, and Google Play: subscription and purchase processing, entitlement checks, and purchase restoration.
  • Vercel: hosting and delivery of the CookBk website and web app.
  • Cloudflare and email delivery providers: domain services and delivery of messages sent to our support address.

The web app may request fonts from Google Fonts. Images or links from Instagram or other external sources connect your device to those sources when loaded or opened, which may disclose your IP address and technical request information under their own privacy practices.

We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law or legal process, investigate misuse, protect rights and safety, or carry out a business transfer, subject to applicable privacy obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or use it for targeted advertising. We do not use advertising SDKs or separate analytics or crash-reporting services in the current release. Service providers still process technical and operational information as described above.

Device access and local storage

CookBk requests camera or photo access when you use a feature that needs it. You can manage permissions in your device settings. We use browser or device storage for sign-in sessions, chat history, preferences, and some recipe or share information. You choose which photos and text to submit. Camera or photo permission does not itself explain or authorize all AI processing; the information submitted for AI features is handled as described in this policy.

Retention and account deletion

Account content. We generally keep your account and saved recipes while your account remains open, including during periods of inactivity, so you can return to your library. We do not currently run automatic account deletion based on inactivity. You can delete individual recipes or delete your account. Deleting a recipe or removing or replacing its uploaded photo triggers removal of the old uploaded photo when another saved recipe does not reference it. Failed cleanup may require a retry; deleting an account removes the user's entire uploaded-photo folder.

Delete your account. Use Profile > Delete account, or visit the CookBk account-deletion page, sign in, and confirm deletion. The process removes your Supabase account, saved recipe records, uploaded photos including abandoned uploads, subscription-access records and daily usage records, and requests deletion of the linked RevenueCat customer. If deletion fails, contact support@cookbk.app. Deletion may have partially completed before an error; contact us if a retry does not resolve it.

Subscriptions. Deleting your CookBk account does not cancel an active App Store or Google Play subscription. Cancel separately through the store where you subscribed. Store transaction records are subject to the store's retention practices.

Local copies. Chat history and other local data can remain on your device or browser until cleared. After successful account deletion, the app attempts to clear local chat history, selected preferences, and the session on that device. Copies on other devices, previously downloaded or exported content, browser caches, and device backups are not necessarily removed by cloud account deletion. Clear local app or browser data on those devices as needed. Uninstalling the app alone does not delete your cloud account.

Supabase backups and logs. Our Supabase Pro plan provides seven days of daily database backups and seven-day standard platform log retention. Previously created backups may contain deleted database records until those backups expire. These database backups contain photo metadata, not the uploaded photo files themselves. We do not currently use Point-in-Time Recovery or Log Drains. The seven-day platform log period does not describe every security, audit, billing, or legal record held by a provider.

Google Gemini. Google documents 55-day retention of prompts, context, and outputs for abuse monitoring, safety, and related legal or regulatory purposes. Optional developer request logging is disabled for CookBk. Deleting local chats or your CookBk account does not itself delete Google's separate abuse-monitoring records.

Apify. Our Free-plan account uses unnamed datasets, key-value stores, and request queues. The configured ordinary retention period is seven days; Apify documents an exception retaining the ten most recent Free-plan runs for four months. We therefore do not promise that all import-processing data is erased after seven days. Account deletion does not directly delete individual Apify run storage. See Apify's retention rules.

Support and necessary records. We retain resolved support conversations for 12 months after resolution, except when longer retention is necessary for an ongoing dispute, security issue, or legal obligation. Providers may retain limited backup, security, billing, or legal records under their own obligations and agreements; the active-customer deletion request does not establish one universal deadline for every provider-held copy. Contact us if you need help with a specific deletion request.

Your choices and privacy requests

You can edit or delete saved recipes, clear local chat history, manage device permissions, cancel subscriptions through the applicable store, and request account deletion. To request access to, correction of, a copy of, or deletion of your personal information, email support@cookbk.app. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. Additional rights, including an appeal where applicable, depend on where you live and which laws apply. You can contact the same address to ask us to review a decision about your request. We will not discriminate against you for exercising applicable privacy rights.

Security and processing locations

We use HTTPS for hosted app and backend connections, authenticated account access, and database and storage access policies that restrict ordinary user access to the user's own content. No internet service or storage method can guarantee absolute security.

Our primary Supabase project is hosted in the US East region. Other service providers may process information in the United States and other countries where they or their subprocessors operate. A US-only launch does not mean every provider processes every request solely in the United States.

Age eligibility

CookBk is intended for users aged 18 and older and is not directed to children or teenagers under 18. If you believe someone under 18 has provided personal information to CookBk, contact support@cookbk.app so we can investigate and take appropriate steps, including deleting the account and associated information.

Changes to this policy

We will update this policy as our services or data practices change. When a revised policy is published, we will identify its effective date. For material changes, we will provide a notice in the app or by email before the changes take effect and obtain consent where required.
